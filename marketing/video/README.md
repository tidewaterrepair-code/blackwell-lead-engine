# AI video scripts

Twelve short-form scripts for Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and (for 09 and 12) a 16:9 cut. Each script has:
- **Hook (0–3 s)**: the line that stops the scroll
- **Shot list**: per-scene timing, visual, voiceover (VO) and on-screen text
- **AI prompts**: paste into a text-to-video model (Higgsfield, Kling, Veo, Runway, etc.) for b-roll; app screens are real screen recordings from `pegasus demo` data
- **Caption + CTA**

Production rules:
- App footage: record the real app (seed with `pegasus demo` and blur house numbers). Never fake UI numbers in a way that implies real customer results.
- No AI-generated "customers" giving testimonials. Real testimonials only, with written permission.
- Voice: confident, plain, fast. 150–170 words per minute.
- Captions burned in (Inter Bold, white with midnight 70% box, gold highlight on the key word).
- Music: royalty-free, punchy (YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay, or the platform's licensed library).
- End card on every video: logo lockup + "Start free · getpegasus.org" + founding-seat line.
