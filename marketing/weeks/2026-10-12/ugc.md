# UGC · week of Oct 12–18, 2026

User-generated-style video is the highest-converting format for contractors and agents: a real person, a phone, a truck or a kitchen, talking straight to camera. This file has the creator brief, six scripts and your own founder-led videos for the week.

## Rules that keep the ad accounts safe

1. **Real people, real opinions.** Creators say what they actually think after using Pegasus on their own area (give them a free account a few days before filming). Never script a result they didn't get ("I closed 5 jobs").
2. **Disclose.** Paid or gifted creators must say it and tag it: Instagram/Facebook *Paid partnership* label, TikTok *Branded content* toggle, plus `#ad` in the caption. This is an FTC requirement, and Meta and TikTok enforce it.
3. **No AI "customers."** AI avatars or AI voices must never be presented as Pegasus customers or real contractors. If you use an AI presenter, it speaks as Pegasus and is labeled as AI.
4. **Screens show demo data** (or the creator's own account with addresses blurred). Never show a homeowner's name, phone or full address on camera.
5. **Usage rights:** ask for 90 days of paid usage (whitelisting/Spark Ads) and organic reposting. Typical rate for micro-creators with 5–50k followers: $150–$400 per video, plus the free account.

## Creator brief (send this to each creator)

> **Product:** Pegasus finds construction and real estate leads from public records (permits, bids, sales, storms, tax and court filings), ranks them for your trade every morning at 7am, and predicts the next jobs. 14 days free at getpegasus.org.
> **Your account:** we've set you up with Growth on your own area. Use it for at least 3 mornings before you film.
> **Deliverable:** one 30–45 s vertical video (9:16, 1080×1920, natural light, phone audio is fine; lav mic if outside), plus 3 alternate first lines (hooks) recorded separately, plus 10 s of silent B-roll of you using the app.
> **Must include:** your honest take; one real moment from your account (with addresses blurred); "14 days free, link in bio/caption"; `#ad`.
> **Avoid:** income or job-count claims you can't back up, competitor bashing by name, showing homeowner personal info, music with lyrics (we add licensed music).
> **Where to find creators:** contractors and agents who already post on TikTok/Instagram in your launch metro (search #deckbuilder #roofer #realtorlife + city), local REIA members, or marketplaces like Billo or Insense.
