Telemarketing and messaging

You must comply with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act, the Telemarketing Sales Rule, state telemarketing laws, CAN-SPAM and carrier messaging rules. Do not call or text numbers flagged Do Not Call or litigator. Don't use autodialers or prerecorded messages to call cell phones without prior express written consent. Honor every opt-out immediately.

Fair housing and anti-discrimination

You may not use Pegasus to target, exclude or treat people differently based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, disability or any other characteristic protected by federal, state or local law. Pegasus scores use property and public-record facts only.

No consumer reports

Pegasus is not a consumer reporting agency. You may not use the Service to determine eligibility for credit, insurance, employment, housing or tenancy, or for any purpose under the Fair Credit Reporting Act.

Respectful outreach to distressed owners

Contact owners facing foreclosure, probate, tax delinquency or eviction with care. Follow state foreclosure-rescue and wholesaling laws, never misrepresent who you are, and don't pressure people in hardship.

No abuse

Don't scrape, resell or redistribute Pegasus data, share accounts beyond your seats, probe or overload the Service, or use it for harassment, stalking or any unlawful purpose.

We may suspend accounts that violate this policy.
